### Decision and next phase
H6B accepted as isolated capacity investigation, not hosted or production readiness. Both independent DB trial sets show 20 connections worse than5, all transactions pass; resource and lock measures remain sparse/container-wide. Current bottleneck for modeled growth is admission budget and repeated read amplification rather than demonstrated CPU exhaustion. Keep H6A 70% review threshold 420 auth/840 total per warm process, 80% 480/960 priority review and unexpected429 trigger; real-clock 8/s (480/min) passed in fixture, not proof headroom for writes/wakes. 1–5 shop scenario no lab evidence requiring upgrade. 25 shops with225 foreground/35% crosses existing budget before order writes; require real non-invasive usage evidence and capacity correction design before expansion. 100 shops/500 foreground requires deliberate admission architecture. Do not buy new resources solely from synthetic results; review hosted usage/quotas/cost and observed DB connections/CPU/memory/latency first. H6C recommendation: isolated design and comparison of reducing per-poll protected HTTP/auth amplification (e.g. combined recipient-scoped history/inbox only after preserving independent stale/error behavior) and safe critical traffic admission within unchanged shared caps, plus query/lease claim cost and bounded connection pooling. No runtime implementation unless targeted reproduction and strict independent acceptance; no suppression of critical missed-push fallback, bypass or blind limit raises.
