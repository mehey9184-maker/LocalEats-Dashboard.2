# LocalPay R4.5 — trusted evidence records, verification process and no-live plan

**Date:** 8 November 2026  
**Approval:** Founder requested **R4.5 trusted evidence DESIGN** following R4.4. This is a design/test-only addition in draft Merchant/API PR #124, **not** consent to create real database tables, alter pickup or Customer/Merchant UX, make a bank transfer, issue LocalPay balance, activate real ordering, approve legal terms, deploy, or merge.

## 0. Problem and existing source (verified in repo)

LocalEats currently operates a pickup-only pay-at-shop business design. Existing pickup code is in `localeats-api/src/orders/pickupService.ts`, `localeats-api/src/routes/orders.ts`, `supabase/drafts/pickup_launch_foundation.sql` (historical draft) and `supabase/drafts/pickup_zero_fee.sql`; cross-check future implementation against the **deployed** schema before writing anything. The current order contract records `orders.id`, `user_id`, `shop_id`, `items` JSONB, `status`, `payment_status`, `delivery_type`, `create_intent_hash`, `revision` and other fields; the pickup event schema records `order_id`, `revision`, `command_id`, `kind`, `actor_uid`, `occurred_at`, `details`. `pickup_order_command` binds a trusted shop-owner UID and `p_handover_confirmed` for existing pickup collection; these are **not** independently verified proof of a customer's physical handover, nor proof that cash/card was received.

R4.4 established the following **REAL DATA GAPS**, rather than proving an active integration:

- Customer GET `/api/v1/orders/:id` strips private intent hash and projects event history without `command_id` and `actor_uid`. **This is sensible customer privacy**, but makes the public response unfit for a finance decision.
- A stored menu line and current catalogue do **not** prove the immutable *Rewards eligibility at checkout*. Old orders must not be retroactively given an eligibility flag.
- Current orders do not establish separate immutable proof of Rewards enrolment, campaign version, non-test purchase origin, a dedicated independent customer pickup check, or complete verified refund review.
- `src/rewards/trustedEvidence.ts` / `financeActor.ts` and R4.1–R4.4 are inert design/tests, not connected to real Firebase/financial records. The former R2.1 R125/R200 reward-bundle prototype and +40 Platinum are historical, NOT the founder's latest business rules.

**Rule:** Missing evidence = **`BLOCKED_MISSING_EVIDENCE`**, not zero refunds, presumed customer consent or a made-up payment. No automatic Rewards grants on existing orders.

## 1. Approved business policy that the design MUST preserve

- Up to **12 pilot customers**, each eligible for **one** Bronze food item capped at **R25** after **four genuine purchased, paid-and-collected eligible kota units** (even if all four are in one order, one day). A R24 gift spends one item; there is no second R1 coupon.
- **R500 ceiling**, maximum **R300** possible Bronze promises (12×R25), **R200 unpromised operational buffer**. This is an intended fund, **not proof that R500 exists in a bank account**. No real participant admitted until owner backing, rules, merchants and actual funding are separately approved.
- Future Silver→Gold: **+20 additional paid-collected kota units** (24 total), planned **R80** restricted food credit; Gold→Platinum: **+30 additional** (54 total), planned **R150** restricted food credit. Future Gold and Platinum may use split tender with cash/card at shop, and 36-month per-grant validity, but are **NOT earnable, funded or retroactive under the Bronze-only pilot**.
- The intended brand is **LocalPay Rewards — eligible food credit**, with potential later separate company only after South African name/trademark, payment classification, consumer, privacy and governance reviews. No bank account, deposit, e-money, cash-out, person-to-person transfer, or customer online payment is authorised.

Each financial-relevant entry must be append-only, time-stamped by trusted server, attributable to an authenticated actor/service, and idempotent. Unique constraints, storage access controls and retention policies require their own approved implementation and legal review.

Version change cannot rewrite old qualification. |

### Current contract vs proposed contract — hard decision

`orders.items`, `pickup_order_events`, `shops.owner_id`, `payment_status` and `revision` **are real upstream fields**. The extra programme, original immutable per-line Rewards eligibility, separate customer handover, and complete refund history in this table **do not exist as a verified, integrated production Rewards contract**. A mock flag called `verified_by_server`, a signed-looking JSON, `server-only` label or Firebase UID string is *not* itself authentication.

## 3. Verification process — proposed sequence with explicit stopping points

1. **Founder's release and funding gate:** Confirm real cash backing using bank/reconciliation evidence, current independent owner and merchant commitments, legal terms, campaign version and explicit go-live approval. Otherwise **do not enrol** and do not advertise rewards.
2. **Enrol before purchase:** In a later serializable/locked transaction admit the customer once, reserve up to R25 subject to R500 actual-backed total, max 12 participants, exact campaign and approved shop terms. Log a unique admission ID. Replays return the identical result; competing admissions cannot exceed the funded commitment ceiling.
3. **Freeze purchase-time evidence:** In an approved future **same order-create transaction** record programme version and exact eligible kota line classification based on trusted catalogue at checkout. If no valid active campaign or eligible snapshot, normal pickup can continue **without earning Rewards**. Do not backfill old orders or change present checkout without a new approval.
4. **Shop payment attestation:** Merchant physically receives cash or card-terminal payment; authenticated server verifies revocation and `shops.owner_id` at the action, binds method/amount to authoritative order, records `payment_confirmed` event and separate risk/audit reference. This is an attestation, not independent proof of terminal/bank settlement. Missing/contradictory amounts => Rewards candidate blocked and investigation.
5. **Independent customer handover evidence:** For **future consenting Rewards customers only**, server creates a cryptographically unpredictable, short-lived, single-order challenge or safe QR claim, bound to customer/shop/order/command/revision. Future UI/operating policy remains **OPEN**. Store a server-keyed hash, not plaintext, enforce expiry, attempted-use/guess limits, rate limits, no disclosure in public customer event history and no retry after consume. Existing `collect` with merchant `handover_confirmed:true` alone is **insufficient for Rewards**, but must continue to work for ordinary pickup.
6. **Confirm collected order and stable source:** Private finance reader checks recorded `paid`→`collected` commands, immutable original line snapshots, enrolled version and customer, separate consumed customer proof, full refund/dispute history and current shop role **inside one consistent snapshot/locked boundary**. No browser-supplied status, shop, quantity, credit amount, names or `paid:true` flags.
7. **Produce a read-only decision first:** `PASS_CANDIDATE` means eligible evidence **for consideration** and `canGrant:false` while the programme is inactive. Repeated attempts with the same order/line/source revision return the same candidate, never a balance. Actual money transitions require a later separate, explicitly approved, idempotent, serializable funded-finance transaction.
8. **Refunds, charge disputes and reversals:** A refund report locks the order/credit candidate and creates review status. If refund wins before credit it must block auto-award; if reward was already earned or spent, preserve customer rights, history and merchant payable and route to independent manual dispute/adjustment rather than secretly clawing back a reward. Partial refunds require exact line and quantity reconciliation; fail closed when evidence incomplete.
9. **Merchant reimbursement (separate future gate):** Reward expense becomes a merchant payable only on a verified actual qualifying food gift handover. Owner records only a real external transfer with reconciled bank evidence. Merchant independently acknowledges receipt or disputes it. No double-counting and no offset against separate merchant subscriptions without specifically approved lawful accounting.

## 4. Threats and future acceptance tests

| Attack or operational failure | Required response and test |
| --- | --- |
| A client asserts `paid`, `collected`, `owner_id`, `eligibleKota`, `reward_minor`, a balance or a fake test-user flag | Never accepted; no direct reward write permission; private reader only with server-derived order ID and transaction |
| An old or simulated order / after-the-fact programme enrolment / current shop menu edit | No retroactive eligibility; unknown immutable source means no candidate |
| Two devices use the same pickup challenge or guess a four-digit code | One consumed challenge only; attempt cap, online throttling, expiry and replay denial; prefer a more robust random challenge than a 4-digit secret for monetary benefit |
| Merchant is revoked or shop transferred between payment and collection | Verify current owner and historic action authorisation, isolate transfer exceptions; block candidate pending review |
| Receipt says R100 paid but server order says R65 eligible plus R35 at shop | Distinguish physical customer remainder vs LocalPay promotional portion, require exact evidence per portion, shop payable only R65 in separately funded future programme; no online payment |
| Paid+collected event written, but customer disputes handover or refund appears | Block or review automatic accrual; don't delete audit or silently claw back legitimate credit |
| Delayed/duplicated outbox, database restart, 13th enrolment | Durable idempotency, unique indexes, transactional outbox, serializable/row lock and verified replay tests |
| Missing bank proof, insufficient funded reservations, unknown customer consent or unauthorised client DB role | Fail closed; never auto-issue reward, owe a shop or mark transfer sent |
| Test works with mock Firebase sessions, merchant booleans or a bare `proof_source` string | Mark synthetic verification only; must replace with actual revocation-checked middleware, server-bound authority, real till/collection controls and independently reviewed finance adapters |

## 5. R4.5 exit criteria: what is complete and what is still OPEN

**R4.5 DESIGN complete when** (i) current pickup facts vs missing Rewards records are explicitly mapped; (ii) a record/provenance/role/uniqueness plan is documented; (iii) the end-to-end proposed process is laid out with stop points and refund/dispute decision states; (iv) an **offline, non-writing test-only schema contract** rejects missing or forged records; (v) PR-only CI and existing pickup regressions are checked. Completing that design **does not** mean the actual database/merchant process or identity system meets those requirements.

**Not authorised / not proven:** Actual South African legal review; R500 bank proof; customer consent + terms; participating merchant signoff; customer one-time challenge design choice/UX approval; bank/till independent proof; deployed private schema/RLS/constraints; data retention/POPIA determination; authenticated API read adapter in a truly isolated backend; real programme funding; Gold/Platinum; production execution; any merge.

No live pipeline or UI rollout before new founder decision.
