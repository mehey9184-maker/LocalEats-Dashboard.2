## Severity and audit method
P0 = possible incorrect money/order/account state or exposure; P1 = business-blocking recovery failure; P2 = operational degradation. Severity indicates impact if reproduced, not confirmed exploit/incident. Source observations, existing mechanisms and unverified hypotheses kept distinct. H5A audit is not proof of runtime resilience.

## Proposed bounded sequence and acceptance gates
H5B Customer interrupted submission/cancellation: extract testable recovery controller if necessary; reproduce R01–R04 first; deadline 15s proposed consistent with quote/merchant; preserve exact UID/intent/consent/idempotency; stop on session generation switch before send and after response; preserve ambiguous pending, no cart clear/redirect/prompt until authoritative scoped confirmation; storage unavailable fail before transport. Test lost response before/after commit, same-key replay, changed amount/key, terminal vs malformed errors, timeout auth/transport, account switch/reload and concurrent calls. No auto writes.
H5C Merchant recovery: reproduce R05,R07,R08; bounded cancelable reads, queued wake and ownership gates; same-account stale labels, no cross-account data or inferred state; 15s poll retained.
H5D Provider and push: R06,R09,R10,R12, injected transient/permanent/hanging provider, receipt/inbox authority, lease and retry behavior, recovery under rate limit. No live provider faults; preserve FCM behavior and all gates.
H5E Restart/offline and integrity: R03,R11,R13, synthetic PostgreSQL isolated race/rollback, browser reload/offline/reconnect, service-worker chunk recovery and explicit retry. No hosted DDL, real orders or payment.
H5F combined acceptance: exact pinned Customer/Merchant/API regression including eleven H4E jobs, targeted fault cases, report observed outcomes and all unresolved real-world blockers. H6 not until H5 accepted. Do not claim fixed until a reproducing test and corrected pass exist.
