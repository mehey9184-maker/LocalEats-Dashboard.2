# LocalEats product marketing context

Document version: v2
Last updated: 2026-11-22

## Product and audience

LocalEats helps customers discover local food shops, browse actual menus and use pickup ordering where the server permits it. The Merchant Dashboard helps shop owners manage their shop and prepare simple menu-sharing materials. The initial owner-approved pilot prioritises township food shops, including kota shops, with genuine device location and a maximum 4 km ordering radius. Out-of-range browsing must never imply ordering eligibility.

## Approved message boundaries

- Pickup only; pay at the physical shop by cash or an available physical card machine.
- Actual API menu prices; no additional pickup platform service fee or delivery fee. Do not invent an offer, item, availability or discount.
- Ordering availability, ownership and payment facts come from the API. Printed materials cannot activate any of them.
- The verified public address for this work is https://www.localeatssa.co.za. Do not substitute localeats.co.za without separate domain verification.
- No delivery promises, online-payment claims, guaranteed preparation times, fabricated customer counts or automatic marketing-consent assumptions.
- Draft, copied, downloaded, published, sent and redeemed describe different outcomes. Label the outcome that actually occurred.

## Brand and conversion goal

Preserve the original Dashboard navigation, LocalEats identity and coral/charcoal palette. Use short, concrete, friendly English. Prefer the merchant's actual shop and item names. For counter signs, the immediate action is to scan and browse the shop menu; ordering is a later action subject to location and shop availability. Keep a readable typed website option above the QR.

## Resource limits

No additional paid infrastructure, AI provider, advertising account, SMS gateway or automated sender is required for the draft/copy/print tools. The agent prepares and tests improvements; merchants review and share materials themselves.

## Guidance source and limits

Applied guidance from https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills at reviewed commit 5b2b400eec1e7e1cec76245a715d0b0d4fd2a9c1: product-marketing context, truthful copywriting, social content adaptation and analytics definitions. These are development instructions, not live product integrations. Do not copy international legal guidance or generic conversion benchmarks into LocalEats claims.

## Counter-sign style

The owner supplied a shopping-bag QR reference and requested a creative food-related replacement. Use the LocalEats serving-dome frame, LocalEats base name, dotted QR with rounded finder eyes and small centred existing orange logo. Keep a classic-pattern fallback and preserve scan reliability over decorative similarity. Never reuse the reference QR payload.
